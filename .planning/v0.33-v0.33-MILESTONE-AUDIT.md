## Integration Observations and Disposition

### Commit-scoped local evidence

The checked-in source and fixture identities still match the most recent local
records, but the records intentionally attest the exact commit at which the
runner generated them. Documentation-only commits made afterward therefore
make strict current-HEAD read-back reject the records. This is expected,
fail-closed behavior rather than a broken product flow: CI always regenerates a
fresh `ci-font-v2` directory for the checked-out revision, and maintainers can
regenerate `font-v2` whenever a new local exact-HEAD attestation is needed.

### Planning literals

The audit corrected the completed-roadmap status and the stale hostile-corpus
accessor pattern. Earlier SUMMARY/REVIEW semantic hashes remain historical
execution records; the final VERIFICATION explicitly treats them as
superseded and revalidated the then-current exact-HEAD comparison instead of
using those historical literals as proof.

### Retained bookkeeping

`FontCollection` retains protected ranges and cached directory facts after open
even though selected admission does not consume every field. This is the
verified compact accounting contract, not an orphaned export or correctness
gap; changing it would be a future optimization with compatibility and budget
implications.
