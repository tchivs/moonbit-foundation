From the production empty tracer:
- `ResourceCharge::checked_add` returns a fresh complete charge without mutating Budget.

- `Font::glyph_for_scalar(Int) -> Result[GlyphId, CoreError]`

- `GlyphId::value() -> UInt64`
- `horizontal_metrics`, both `kerning` operands, and `outline` consume GlyphId while retaining their current public signatures.
