<interfaces>
`checked_compose_affine(current, other) -> Result[Affine2, CoreError]` is the package-private authority for accumulated transform admission. `lower_to_drawing_list(SceneNode) -> DrawingList` remains public and total.

The lowering walker must carry a safe accumulated affine from the initial identity through each Svg viewBox map and Group transform. At a Group boundary, compose `current` with the supplied transform; on success record the supplied local transform and pass the checked composed value to its children. On failure record identity for that Group and pass the recovered safe `current` to its children. This preserves the DrawingList's stack-relative transform representation without allowing the failed local contribution into the child effective state.

`parse_svg(source) -> Result[SceneNode, CoreError]` already rejects unsafe parsed Group transforms before SceneNode publication. Preserve that parser-only error behavior; the manual-value recovery must not alter parser control flow or expose a Result in the public lowerer API.
</interfaces>
