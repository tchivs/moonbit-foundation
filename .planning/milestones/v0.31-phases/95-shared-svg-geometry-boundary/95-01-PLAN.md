<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| SVG source to `parse_svg` | Untrusted numeric source and derived geometry must be rejected before a scene is published. |
| Public `SceneNode` to `lower_to_drawing_list` | Callers may manually construct values that parser admission would not produce. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-95-01 | Tampering | `geometry.mbt` checked derivation | high | mitigate | Every derived viewBox, affine, point, and path fact returns the established CoreError through `admit_derived`; `scene.mbt` propagates it before publication. |
| T-95-02 | Denial of Service | `lower_to_drawing_list` with a manually constructed invalid `SceneNode` | medium | mitigate | Private total adapters use identity or empty Path2 recovery and keep public lowering non-panicking. |
| T-95-03 | Tampering | geometry/layer boundary | medium | mitigate | Keep style creation and PushLayer/PopLayer sequencing in `lower.mbt`; later compatibility controls retain opacity and layer-depth semantics. |
</threat_model>
