# v0.32 Milestone Audit — TrueType Font Foundation

## Verdict: PASSED

All five v0.32 requirements are satisfied. Phases 97–100 form one portable
`tchivs/mb-font` contract rather than four disconnected feature slices:
bounded admission retains the table and metric facts consumed by deterministic
Unicode mapping and kerning, those opaque glyph identities feed transactional
simple/composite outline extraction, and Phase 100 exercises the complete public
workflow plus hostile boundaries against immutable compact and licensed
DejaVu Sans fixtures on every supported target.

end-to-end flow, blocking gap, or milestone-scope technical debt remains.
